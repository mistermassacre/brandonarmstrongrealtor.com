PCS to Fort Carson: A VA Loan Home Buying Guide for Colorado Springs

By Brandon Armstrong, REALTOR®, The Front Range Team, Real Broker, LLC · October 26, 2026

If you have PCS orders to Fort Carson, you can buy a home in Colorado Springs with a VA-backed loan, and many buyers do it with no down payment. The steps that matter most are requesting your Certificate of Eligibility early, getting pre-approved with a lender who closes VA loans regularly, and timing your search around your report date. Here's how I walk military buyers through it.

VA loan basics for Fort Carson buyers

A VA-backed purchase loan is made by a private lender and backed by the VA. According to the VA's purchase loan page, you may be eligible if you qualify for a Certificate of Eligibility (COE), meet the VA's and your lender's standards for credit and income, and plan to live in the home. The same page says these loans often offer:

  • No down payment, as long as the sales price isn't higher than the appraised value
  • No private mortgage insurance (PMI)
  • Fewer closing costs, which the seller may pay
  • No penalty for paying the loan off early

The VA doesn't set your interest rate. Your lender does, so it's worth comparing more than one lender before you commit.

Start with your COE

Your COE confirms to the lender that you qualify for the VA home loan benefit. The VA's COE page says you can request it online, through your lender, or by mail. Active-duty service members need a statement of service signed by their commander, adjutant, or personnel officer. Veterans need their DD214. If you can, get this done before you leave your current duty station.

Understand the funding fee

Most VA buyers pay a one-time VA funding fee, which you can pay at closing or roll into the loan. The VA's funding fee page says the fee is a percentage of the loan amount based on your loan type, whether it's your first use, and your down payment. As of the VA's current chart, a first-use purchase loan with less than 5% down carries a 2.15% fee. Buyers who receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability are among those who don't pay it.

The same page says the VA limits seller concessions to 4% of the home's reasonable value, and that the funding fee can be financed into the loan or paid at closing. Your lender's Loan Estimate is the place to confirm your actual numbers.

Planning your search around your orders

Fort Carson sits on the south side of Colorado Springs, and its relocation team notes that more than 60% of the active-duty military population lives in the local communities. That gives you real options, from neighborhoods on the south and southeast side of the Springs to the City of Fountain and areas farther north if you're willing to commute.

Here's the order I usually recommend:

  1. Get your COE and a VA pre-approval as soon as your orders are in hand.
  2. Figure out your move-in window, including any time you'll spend in temporary lodging.
  3. Start touring remotely with video walkthroughs so you're ready to act when you arrive.
  4. Line up inspections and the VA appraisal with closing dates that fit your report date.

Fort Carson's Army Community Service also runs a monthly virtual homebuyers workshop, and the Housing Services Office on post can point you to rental and housing resources. Both are worth using alongside your own agent and lender.

Questions to ask before you make an offer

These are the questions I go over with every PCS buyer:

  • How long do I expect to be stationed here, and would I keep this home as a rental after my next move? (Ask your lender and a tax professional about the rules that apply to you.)
  • What will my commute to my gate actually look like at rush hour?
  • Does the home need repairs that could hold up the VA appraisal?
  • Is the home in an HOA or special district, and what do those fees cover?

You can read more on my military and PCS page or reach out when your orders come through.

VA loan rules can change. Confirm your eligibility, fees, and loan terms with your lender and VA.gov.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a house in Colorado Springs with a VA loan and no down payment?

Often, yes. The VA says a VA-backed purchase loan often requires no down payment as long as the sales price isn't higher than the appraised value, but you still have to meet VA and lender standards for credit and income. Confirm the details with your lender.

How do I get my VA Certificate of Eligibility (COE) as an active-duty soldier?

You can request it online, through your lender, or by mail. Active-duty service members need a statement of service signed by their commander, adjutant, or personnel officer, according to VA.gov.

Do I have to pay the VA funding fee?

Most buyers do, but the VA lists exemptions, including people who receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability and active-duty members who show proof of a Purple Heart on or before closing. Your lender can confirm whether you're exempt.

Do I have to live on post at Fort Carson?

Not necessarily. Fort Carson's relocation team says more than 60% of the active-duty military population lives in the local communities. Check your own orders and unit requirements, since some soldiers are required to live in the barracks.

Bottom Line

If you have PCS orders to Fort Carson, request your COE early, get pre-approved with a lender who knows VA loans, and plan your search around your report date. I'm happy to help you map out the move.
