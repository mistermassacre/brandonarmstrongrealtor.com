How buying works with me

  1. Clarify goals. We go over budget, timeline, and neighborhood must-haves, plus PCS dates if that applies to you.
  2. Search with a plan. You see listings that fit, including new-construction builder connections and off-market homes when they make sense.
  3. Tour with intent. Showings stay focused, and I explain the tradeoffs in plain language.
  4. Offer and negotiate. I write competitive offers without pushing you to overreach.
  5. Close with support. I stay on top of deadlines and inspections and help you all the way to the keys.
