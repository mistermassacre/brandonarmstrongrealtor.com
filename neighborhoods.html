Colorado Springs neighborhoods

I match each neighborhood to how you live, including commute, schools, and lifestyle.

I help buyers in Colorado Springs and across the Front Range, including Briargate, Broadmoor, and other Springs communities.

Briargate

An established area on the north side of Colorado Springs with a mix of home styles and neighborhood parks.

Broadmoor

A long-standing area in southwest Colorado Springs near the foothills of Cheyenne Mountain.

Northgate & north Springs

Communities on the city's north end, convenient to I-25 and the U.S. Air Force Academy area.

East Colorado Springs

Growing areas on the east side, often considered by buyers commuting to Peterson and Schriever.

Near Fort Carson

Stationed at Fort Carson? Homes in south Colorado Springs, Fountain, and Security-Widefield put you within commuting distance of the post. I plan the search around your PCS timeline and report date, with VA loans in mind.
